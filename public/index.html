Pulse

Pulse is a visual synthesizer. It turns the audio playing on your computer into live visuals, and phones connected over WebSocket add their own rhythm, colour and motion on top.

How it works
  1. The display reads your desktop audio. Open it on your computer and give the site permission to share system audio. The music's waveform and spectrum drive the visuals.
  2. Phones send extra data. Each phone that joins streams to the display over WebSocket. Its touch pad has a toggle: Paint changes the colour and Stir moves the motion.
  3. Your heartbeat sets the tempo. The phone's heart reader uses the camera to measure your pulse and turns it into a BPM. That beat works together with the audio. You can also tap or dance to set the beat instead.

Phones can also switch visual modes, effects and sliders like Chaos, Glow and Speed, unless the host has locked them.
